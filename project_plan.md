# Project plan: "bank" flight mode

A new flight mode, **"bank"**. Development conventions (target independence, compile-time gating, tests, PR rules) are in [AGENTS.md](AGENTS.md).

Goal: fly a drone like a car on a banked track. The three control axes are coupled down to two, with a spare stick axis for look-around. Mode 2 stick mapping:

| Stick | Channel | Function |
|---|---|---|
| Left U/D | throttle | **forward speed command**, not raw throttle: bottom = zero speed (hard brake, and the pose for arming), top = the steady speed at the maximum tilt. No reverse. Speed is estimated from rotor drag (accelerometer) and attitude, in g of drag; control is feedforward + P (`bank_speed_kp`, `bank_speed_deadband`), so the bottom of the stick auto-brakes to a stop. Tilting forward also raises collective thrust so the vertical component is unchanged |
| Left L/R | yaw | **bank** left/right = turn in the horizontal plane |
| Right U/D | pitch | **vertical lift command**. Bottom = no lift at all (free fall), centre = 1 g (thrust balances weight), top = maximum thrust entirely as lift, whatever multiple of the weight that is. The thrust-to-weight ratio is learnt in flight from the body-Z accelerometer (thrust per unit mass, in g, whatever the tilt) over the collective, near 1 g of thrust only, so the centre holds the vertical acceleration at zero without guessing it; `bank_thrust_to_weight` is the starting value and the estimate stays within ±30% of it. The centre stops vertical acceleration, not vertical speed: that is not observable without baro |
| Right L/R | roll | **head check**: yaw the nose away from the direction of travel *without* changing the direction of travel; springs back to neutral when the stick centres |

Design decisions so far:
- **Attitude only** (gyro/ACC); no GPS or baro required.
- Keep an internal virtual *travel heading* (a course reference, similar in spirit to headfree). Nose yaw = travel heading + head-check offset; the head-check offset must not affect the velocity direction.
- **Force-vector control (decoupled axes)**: the sticks command a net force vector, and the mixer of attitude + collective realises it. Required collective for tilt angle θ (pitch) and φ (bank) is T = (m·g + F_z) / (cos θ · cos φ), so with the right stick at the hover position, moving left U/D up from the bottom pitches the craft and increases throttle by 1/cos θ, giving forward force without changing vertical force. The right stick sets the vertical term F_z, with the bottom of the stick giving no lift. Arming is with the left stick at the bottom (zero speed, no tilt). Collective is therefore always computed from the commanded tilt, never taken directly from the stick.
- **Coordinated banked turn**: the yaw stick commands bank angle φ and the mode applies the same 1/cos tilt compensation so the vertical force stays balanced. The yaw is generated by a **"ball" loop** like the one in an aviation turn-and-slip indicator. tan φ = v·ω / g cannot be solved for ω without v, so instead of computing it, yaw is driven (P + I, with the sign flipped for rearward travel) to null the lateral specific force seen by the accelerometer, which on a multirotor is rotor drag and so a direct slip signal. The ball centred is the same condition as a perfect bank, with v eliminated and no velocity estimate. The settings are `bank_ball_kp`, `bank_ball_ki` and `bank_ball_deadband`.

Open questions (decide with the user before implementing): mapping from left U/D to forward force / tilt angle, and from right U/D to vertical force (scaling, deadband around neutral), thrust saturation limits when tilt compensation demands more than max throttle; max bank angle and head-check range / return rate; interaction with arming, failsafe and ANGLE/HORIZON/HEADFREE/ALT_HOLD/POS_HOLD priority.

Likely touchpoints (use `pos_hold_multirotor.c`, `alt_hold_multirotor.c` and `poshold_unittest.cc` / `althold_unittest.cc` as the closest patterns):
- `fc/rc_modes.h` (new `BOXBANK` before `BOXID_FLIGHTMODE_LAST`), `fc/runtime_config.h` (new `BANK_MODE` bit + map entry), `msp/msp_box.c` (box name, new unique `permanentId`, availability in the box-enable list)
- `fc/core.c` (mode enable/disable, cf. `HEADFREE_MODE`), `fc/rc.c` (setpoint shaping, cf. the `HEADFREE_MODE` block), `flight/imu.c` (cf. headfree attitude handling), `flight/pid.c`, `flight/mixer.c` (thrust compensation)
- new `flight/bank_*.c/h` with a PG config (`pg/pg_ids.h`, `cli/settings.c`), gated by a new `USE_BANK_MODE` define in `target/common_post.h`
- mode indicators: `osd/osd_elements.c`, `osd/osd_warnings.c`, `io/ledstrip.c`, `telemetry/*`
- `src/test/unit/bank_unittest.cc` plus `bank_unittest_SRC` in `src/test/Makefile`
