# Project plan: "bank" flight mode

A new flight mode, **"bank"**. Development conventions (target independence, compile-time gating, tests, PR rules) are in [CLAUDE.md](CLAUDE.md).

Goal: fly a drone like a car on a banked track. The three control axes are coupled down to two, with a spare stick axis for look-around.

## Terms

Agreed with the owner on 2026-10-16. Use these words, in these meanings, when talking about the mode, in code comments and in reviews. The terms set the vocabulary, not the length: code comments stay short, as CLAUDE.md's Conventions say.

**Two frames of reference.**
- **World frame**: fixed to the ground. Up is against gravity, and the horizontal plane is the plane the craft drives in, like the surface of a banked track. Bank mode is mostly defined here: the pilot asks for what the craft should do in the world, not for an attitude.
- **Drone frame** (body frame): fixed to the craft. Its axes are the nose (forward), the left side and the top, so it tilts and turns with the craft. The props push along the drone frame's top axis, and the accelerometer and gyro measure in this frame.

**The three controls (world frame).** In bank mode the sticks are called by what they control, not by their channel names:
- **Speed** (left stick up and down, the throttle channel): forward motion along the direction of travel, horizontal in the world frame. The lower half of the stick asks for a forward *velocity*: the mode estimates the speed from the rotor drag the accelerometer measures and drives it to the stick with a speed loop, so Speed at the bottom brakes to a stop, relative to the air (the same as relative to the ground when there is no wind). The upper half asks for a forward *force*, from what the centre gives up to all the thrust allows at the top. Below `min_check` a small reverse (see "Reverse").
- **Forward force**: the forward, horizontal, world-frame component of the thrust, in g. The speed loop sets it in the lower half of the Speed stick; the stick sets it directly in the upper half.
- **Lift** (right stick up and down, the pitch channel): the vertical force in the world frame, in multiples of the craft's weight (g). It is a force, not a speed or a height: 1 g at the centre balances the weight, so the craft has no vertical acceleration, and whatever vertical velocity it already has carries on. See "Lift stays a force" below.
- **Bank** (left stick left and right, the yaw channel): the bank angle of the turn, and with it the sideways force in the world frame that curves the track. The nose is turned to follow the turn by itself (the ball, below), and looks into the turn, further the more Bank against Speed (see "Looking into the turn").
- **Head check** (right stick left and right, the roll channel): turns the nose away from the direction of travel without changing the direction of travel. It is the one control that is about the drone frame: it moves the nose, not the track.

**Thrust (drone frame).** The absolute force out of the props, along the drone frame's top axis. It is the one force the craft can make, so Lift, the forward force that holds the Speed and the sideways force of the Bank are all components of the thrust, seen in the world frame. Tilting the craft points the thrust: tilting forward by the pitch θ turns part of it into forward force, banking by φ turns part of it sideways, and what is left points up as Lift:

    Thrust = Lift / (cos θ · cos φ)

So with Lift at 1 g and Speed pushed up, the craft pitches forward and the thrust rises by 1 / cos θ so that Lift stays at 1 g. The same formula holds at every Speed, Lift and Bank.

**Other words used in this plan and in `bank_mode.c`.**
- **Collective**: the command to the motors, 0 to 1, that sets the thrust (the same for all motors before the attitude loop adds its differences). The mode turns a thrust into a collective with the thrust curve `thrust = bank_thrust_to_weight * collective^1.5`.
- **Thrust to weight** (T/W, `bank_thrust_to_weight`): the thrust at full collective, in multiples of the weight. Fixed by a setting, measured in a hover.
- **Thrust trim**: a factor on the modelled thrust, measured in flight from the accelerometer's vertical force against the Lift asked for. Real props make more or less thrust per collective than the fixed model (more when braking into the air, much less in fast forward flight), and the trim corrects for that, so the Lift asked for is the Lift the craft gets. (A version without it was tried on 2026-10-18 and parked, see "No thrust trim".)
- **g of drag**: the unit of the speed estimate. The rotor drag, in g, the craft would have at that speed at 1 g of thrust. In steady flight it is the forward force that holds the speed, which is why no m/s calibration is needed. On the simulator's five inch 1.0 g of drag is about 40 m/s.
- **Direction of travel** (track, travel heading): the direction the craft moves in the horizontal plane. Speed is along it, Bank curves it, and the head check offsets the nose from it.
- **Look dot** and **look turn** (owner, 2026-10-17): where the nose points in a turn. Look dot is along the direction of travel, like a car on a banked track. Look turn is into the turn, along the ground-referenced direction of the forward and turning forces the sticks ask for, like a drifting car (today taken from the sticks, `atan2(Bank, Speed)`, see "Looking into the turn"). The two differ only by a yaw offset, so `bank_look_turn` (0 to 100 %) scales it: 0 is look dot, 100 is look turn. The thrust in the world frame, and so the track, is the same at any value; only what the firmware measures changes (see "Held full banks", "Still true" (1)).
- **The ball**: the sideways force the accelerometer measures in the direction of travel, like the ball of an aircraft's turn-and-slip indicator. Centred, the turn is coordinated: the nose follows the track and the craft neither slips nor skids. The yaw is driven to keep it centred.
- **Thrust priority**: when Lift, Bank and Speed together need more thrust than the props can give, Lift is kept first, Bank second, and Speed gives way. A brake is the exception: it comes before Bank (see "Brake before Bank").

Mode 2 stick mapping (left and right swapped back on 2026-10-18 by the owner: Bank on the left stick, the head check on the right; flight logs from before then banked on the roll channel):

| Stick | Channel | Function |
|---|---|---|
| Left U/D | throttle | **Speed**, not raw throttle. Lower half = a speed: bottom = zero speed (hard brake, and the pose for arming) up to `bank_mid_speed` (default 0.50 g of drag, about 20 m/s on the five inch) at the centre; only the bottom third brakes a craft that is faster than the stick's speed, above it the craft coasts down by its own drag. Upper half = a forward force: a straight line from the force the centre gives to all the (trimmed) thrust allows at the top, so full stick tilts as far as the thrust allows, as at full throttle in acro, and both modes reach the same top speed. The force over the stick is continuous at the centre. Forward tilt is limited only by the thrust; tilting back to brake by 30 degrees, and only within what the bank leaves. The stop is a band over the first 10 % of the stick above `min_check`; below `min_check` a small reverse, once the stick has been raised since arming (see "Reverse"). Speed is estimated from rotor drag (accelerometer) and attitude, in g of drag; control is feedforward + P (gain 2, deadband 0.02 g, constants since the rewrite), so the bottom of the stick auto-brakes to a stop. Tilting forward also raises the thrust so Lift is unchanged |
| Left L/R | yaw | **Bank** left/right = turn in the horizontal plane. The stick is the bank angle as a share of 90 degrees: full stick is as steep as the thrust reserve allows (since the rewrite there is no setting, and no limit from the yaw rate) |
| Right U/D | pitch | **Lift**, the vertical force command. Bottom = a floor of 0.2 g (`BANK_LIFT_FLOOR`, since 2026-10-18, see "Zero Lift locks up"; it was 0 g, free fall), centre = 1 g (thrust balances weight), top = maximum thrust entirely as lift, whatever multiple of the weight that is. Linear below the centre; above it `1 + 0.8 x + (T/W - 1.8) x^3`, so both halves start at the same slope (0.8 g per half stick). The thrust-to-weight ratio is the fixed setting `bank_thrust_to_weight` (default 5.70, the simulator's five inch measured in a hover on a full pack), through a thrust curve `T = T/W * collective^1.5`; nothing is learnt in flight. The stick is a plain force with no vertical speed estimate: at the centre a climb or a sink carries on until the pilot stops it, and neither height nor vertical speed is held (the owner's decision, see "Lift stays a force") |
| Right L/R | roll | **head check**: yaw the nose away from the direction of travel *without* changing the direction of travel; springs back to neutral when the stick centres (unused on `bank-race-rewrite` for now, see "Race rewrite") |

Design decisions so far:
- **Flight controls first, the look second** (owner, 2026-10-16): Speed, Lift and Bank always take priority over where the nose looks. The look into the turn and the head check may only rotate the view; they must never delay, weaken or fight what the flight controls ask for, and when the two compete (yaw authority, thrust, the attitude loop's attention) the flight controls win and the look waits. Check every change to the look against this.
- **Attitude only** (gyro/ACC); no GPS or baro required.
- Keep an internal virtual *travel heading* (a course reference, similar in spirit to headfree). Nose yaw = travel heading + head-check offset; the head-check offset must not affect the velocity direction.
- **Force-vector control (decoupled axes)**: the sticks command a net force vector, and the mixer of attitude + collective realises it. Required collective for tilt angle θ (pitch) and φ (bank) is T = (m·g + F_z) / (cos θ · cos φ), so with the right stick at the hover position, moving left U/D up from the bottom pitches the craft and increases throttle by 1/cos θ, giving forward force without changing vertical force. The right stick sets the vertical term F_z, with the bottom of the stick giving no lift. Arming is with the left stick at the bottom (zero speed, no tilt). Collective is therefore always computed from the tilt, never taken directly from the stick: from the measured tilt (since 2026-10-16), so Lift is right while the craft is still rotating to the commanded one.
- **Coordinated banked turn**: the yaw stick commands bank angle φ and the mode applies the same 1/cos tilt compensation so the vertical force stays balanced. The yaw is generated by a **"ball" loop** like the one in an aviation turn-and-slip indicator. tan φ = v·ω / g cannot be solved for ω without v, so instead of computing it, yaw is driven (P, with the sign flipped for rearward travel) to null the lateral specific force seen by the accelerometer, which on a multirotor is rotor drag and so a direct slip signal. The ball centred is the same condition as a perfect bank, with v eliminated. On top of it the coordinated turn rate is fed forward from the speed estimate (see "Rewrite"). Until the rewrite the ball loop was P + I with the settings `bank_ball_kp`, `bank_ball_ki` and `bank_ball_deadband`.

## Race rewrite (2026-10-19, branch `bank-race-rewrite`, experimental)

The owner asked for the mode to be rebuilt from scratch as a racing mode: as good as perfect acro in straight-line speed, full punch upwards and turn tightness. It is on the branch `bank-race-rewrite` in both repos (the sim's branch has the same name). Where this section and the ones after it disagree, this section holds on that branch; `banked-mode-init` keeps the previous mode.

**Owner decisions (2026-10-19).**
- Full Bank asks for the most sideways force there is, and Lift gives way to get it.
- A hard turn bleeds speed towards a corner speed by itself, as an acro pilot does.
- The Speed stick stays the hybrid (lower half a speed, upper half a force).
- The head check and the look into the turn are dropped for now (to-dos below). The roll stick does nothing.

**Design.** `flight/bank_mode.c` was rewritten, with the old code as a statement of intent only.
- **One thrust vector**, in the travel frame, in priority order:
  1. The corner force. Between 60 % and 85 % of the Bank stick the Speed stick's force is shared into a speed loop towards the corner speed `v = bank_drag_rate * full-Bank force / bank_corner_rate`. It brakes a fast craft and holds the speed of a slow one.
  2. Bank, with Lift giving way down to 80 % of the stick's Lift, then the bank angle giving way.
  3. The Speed stick's forward force or brake from what is left. Brakes go up to 60 degrees back (was 30).
  The vector is turned into the heading frame by the slip, so a lagging nose never moves the force on the track.
- **Flies on rates, like acro.** The mode sets all three body rates itself. The thrust axis is turned towards the vector about the axis between them (20/s per radian, at most 900 deg/s), and the heading's turn rate is shared over the body axes. ANGLE and HORIZON are switched off under BANK. The pid.c hooks are gone, and the rc.c hook sets all three rates.
- **Velocity, estimated in 3D in g of drag.** The accelerometer is integrated in the heading frame (rotated by the gyro's Euler yaw rate) and corrected in the rotor plane by the rotor drag. A climb or sink across tilted rotors no longer reads as slip. The vertical estimate leaks at 0.2/s.
- **One airframe constant, `bank_drag_rate`** (D, 1/s, default 0.40 for the sim's five inch), ties it all together:
  - integration: dv/dt = D * specific force;
  - the track's turn rate: omega = D * sideways force / speed, fed forward to the yaw;
  - the corner speed.
- **Lift.**
  - The collective is set for Lift at the measured tilt.
  - The thrust model's trim is measured along the thrust axis, so it holds at any tilt, with a 0.05 floor: at 37 m/s the props give under a fifth of the modelled thrust.
  - The rotor drag's own vertical part counts towards Lift.
  - An integral on the measured vertical force covers what is left. It runs only while moving, so the ground can't wind it up.
  - The force budget follows the trim at 1 Hz, so at the edge of the envelope the forward tilt gives way smoothly.
- **Speed stick.** Above the bottom third, a craft that is too fast now coasts with no forward force. Before, it kept the feedforward, which pitched craft overrun because the rotor drag partly lifts.
- **Settings.**
  - Removed: `bank_head_check_range`, `bank_look_turn`.
  - Added: `bank_drag_rate` (x100 1/s, default 40) and `bank_corner_rate` (deg/s, default 300).
  - PG version 6.

**Results (sim, five inch at 1.62 g).** `npm run bank:bench` in the sim (`scripts/bank-bench.js`) flies each figure from the same start in bank mode and with an ideal acro pilot. That pilot reads the plant's true state and flies linear rates through a quaternion attitude loop. The bank pilot holds the height with the Lift stick.

| Figure | Bank | Acro | Bank vs acro |
|---|---|---|---|
| Top speed, level | 38.2 m/s | 36.4 m/s | 105 % |
| Punch, height in 1 s | 17.2 m | 17.7 m | 97 % |
| 180 degree reversal from 20 m/s, time | 0.78 s | 0.86 s | 111 % |
| the same, width | 6.9 m | 9.0 m | 131 % |

- The previous mode turned the same reversal in 1.95 s and 23 m, losing 12 m of height.
- `npm run bank:cycle` passes. The head check rows and `turn_ball_g` were removed. `turn_slip_deg` has a band of ±10 degrees and measures 6.9.
- An airborne replay of `webfpv-bank-20261019-113118.csv`'s bank sticks (from 31 s, after a take-off to 20 m) flies without incident, with full-Bank turns at roughly 380 deg/s holding height.

**For the owner to decide.**
1. **Lift gives way by at most a fifth** (`BANK_LIFT_YIELD`). Letting Lift go all the way, at an 85 degree bank, made a held full Bank a 7 m/s spiral dive into the ground in the replay, for 1 % more sideways force on this craft. On a 1.6 T/W craft the fifth is worth about 9 % more force than holding Lift.
2. **The corner force comes before Bank.** At full Bank there is no thrust left over, so a brake or speed hold taken from the remainder never happens. The speed bled to 4 m/s and the turn became a spin.

**To-do.**
- The head check, on the roll stick.
- The look into the turn. The sim's N key does nothing until then.

**Open points.**
- The steady slip in a half-Bank turn is about 7 degrees, with the nose lagging the track. The estimate reads about 1, so it is an estimator bias, probably the airframe's uneven drag, not a loop error.
- `bank_drag_rate` must be found for the Air65. One way is to compare the debug speed with a known speed, or read it from the turn rate of a steady turn: omega * speed / force.
- The attitude loop closes at the RC rate. That is fine at 250 Hz or more; at 50 Hz with a gain of 20/s it is marginal.
- `bank_corner_rate` is untuned.
- `DEBUG_BANK` fields: speed, corner speed, trim, forward force, sideways force, slip (0.1 deg), turn feedforward (0.1 deg/s), Lift - 1.

## Rewrite (2026-10-18)

The owner asked for the mode to be rebuilt from its intent, as simple as possible, accepting slightly less refined control for a large gain in simplicity. The state before it is saved in `.bank-snapshots/2026-10-18-pre-rewrite/` (both repos' diffs, the flown `sim-bank.wasm`, copies of the owner's logs 151904/175027/175231/232749; excluded locally, drop before a PR). The sections after this one record how the previous implementation got there; where they disagree with this section, this section holds.

**Design: one thrust vector.** The sticks set a force vector in the travel frame (x forward along the track, r to the right, z up), in g, and it becomes angles only at the end:
- z = Lift (stick law unchanged), r = Lift · tan(Bank stick × 90 degrees), x = the forward force for the Speed stick (law unchanged: lower half a speed with feedforward + P, braking only in the bottom third; upper half a force up to all there is).
- Thrust priority (Lift, then Bank, then Speed) is one circle clip: Tb = Lift + 0.75 · (trimmed T/W − Lift), H = sqrt(Tb² − Lift²); r takes what it needs of H, x gets sqrt(H² − r²) forwards; a brake (at most 30 degrees back) is taken out of H before r (since 2026-10-18, see "Brake before Bank"). Below 0.5 g of Lift the geometry uses 0.5 g (the nose dive fix); only the collective follows the stick to 0.
- The nose offset (head check + `bank_look_turn` × atan2(Bank, Speed), slewed at 360 deg/s) rotates (x, r) into the nose frame: the world force, and so the track, is the same at any offset.
- pitch target = asin(x / |thrust|), roll target = atan2(r, z); thrust = Lift / max(cos(measured tilt), Lift / Tb), none inverted; collective = (thrust / (T/W · trim))^(2/3).
- Speed: the accelerometer X/Y, levelled with the measured attitude and divided by sqrt(thrust) per sample, 2 Hz, rotated into the travel frame; its size, negative only for clearly rearward drag.
- Yaw: 19 · tan(measured bank, travel frame) / speed (the coordinated turn, within 60 % of 360 deg/s) + 600 · ball + the offset's slew rate. No integrator, no learner. (Since tuning round 2: within 60 % of 720 deg/s, an integral on the ball, the ball faded out with the speed, the drag filtered in the travel frame; see there.)
- Thrust trim as before (measured over modelled vertical force, 10 Hz motor lag, 3 Hz, no measurement saturated / past 75 degrees / below 0.3 g, only back towards 1 at 1 g or more, scaled down with the inflow), but only a trim above 1 leaks (1 s).

The owner flew it on 2026-10-18 (`webfpv-bank-20261018-005929.csv`) and found it feeling better than before.

**How it was decided.** A scratch scorecard (scripted scenarios plus segments of the owner's logs, five inch at 1.62 g from the ground) was flown on the baseline with each mechanism switched off in turn. Kept, because switching it off hurt: the trim and its gates, ground rule, motor lag and inflow scaling; the drag levelling and sqrt(thrust) normalisation; the turn feedforward, from the measured bank; the collective from the measured tilt and its floor at the tilt budget; reverse travel; `BANK_MIN_LIFT`; the pid.c hook sharing the yaw by the measured attitude (without it the nose swings 16 to 25 degrees in a reversal at full Speed). Removed, because nothing scripted moved: the forward tilt slew and rolling freeze, the held bank in the forward tilt budget, the coordinated bank limit with its coordinated speed force, the ball integrator with its rescaling and gates, the turn gain learner, `bankCanReverse` and the braking exception, the slow trim leak. The leak of a trim above 1 was removed and put back: on the ground any trim measures as right, and the motors spinning up walked it up with nothing to bring it back (a unit test found it; the scorecard takes off at once and did not).

**Settings 14 to 4**: `bank_thrust_to_weight`, `bank_mid_speed`, `bank_head_check_range`, `bank_look_turn` (PG version 5). Constants now: bank 90 degrees at full stick, brake 30 degrees, stick deadband 5 %, yaw limit and head check slew 360 deg/s (yaw limit 720 since tuning round 2), ball Kp 600 deg/s per g (and Ki 600 deg/s per g per s since tuning round 2), speed Kp 2 and deadband 0.02 g, turn gain 19. `DEBUG_BANK` 5 is now the turn feedforward (0.1 deg/s), the others as before.

**Size**: `bank_mode.c` 521 to 310 lines of code, 43 to 24 functions, 16 to 9 state fields; the unit test 105 to 30 tests that check properties (Lift kept at any tilt, the head check leaves the world force unchanged, the priority order, ...).

**Measured** (against the baseline): `bank:check` the same rows pass (`turn_ball_g` -0.127 to -0.138, still out of band); head check nose -92.8 to -90.0, return 0.85 to 0.03, track change -5.9 to -5.2. Scripted scenarios within a few percent nearly everywhere; worse: a full bank held at 0.15 Speed has the nose up to 45 degrees off the track (35), a full bank entered at full Speed loses 2.2 m (0.9); better: no pitch target steps with Lift pulled in a bank (9 to 0.05 degrees per frame), a full bank from the hover yaws at 114 deg/s (169). Replays of the owner's logs are chaotic (a 1 degree head check range change moves yaw saturation 1.5 to 8.9 %), so they were read for gross failures only: both builds fly the same class of result, each crashing in one segment the other survives (175231 30 to 40 s: the baseline in all of five perturbed runs, the rewrite in two; 232749 175 to 185 s, a Lift-down dive at full Speed: the rewrite touches the ground, the baseline bottoms out at 4.5 m).

Found while checking, not changed (both builds): (1) full Speed from the hover loses about 2.4 m/s of vertical speed in the first 3 s at any `bank_thrust_to_weight` (the trim lags the props' thrust loss as the speed builds). (2) A wrong `bank_thrust_to_weight` shows mostly at take-off, where the trim starts at 1: the take-off climb at 4.5 / 5.7 / 7.0 is 4.1 / 2.7 / 0.6 m/s; at 7.0 the scripted full Speed sorties then touch the ground, at 4.5 they fly. (3) A head check held into a hard bank at full Speed (175231 60 to 70 s) swings the roll target between sides, the known "look rocks at steep tilt".

## Implemented (2026-10-15)

The history of the previous implementation, kept for what was learnt; see "Rewrite" above for what holds now.


Decisions made while flying it in the simulator, now in `flight/bank_mode.c`:

- **Thrust priority**: lift first, then bank, then forward speed. The tilt gets 75 % of the thrust above the vertical force; the rest is kept so the attitude loop still has motors to work with at the limit (without it a full bank at medium speed rolled over in the simulator).
- **Bank limit**: the bank is also capped at the steepest coordinated turn the yaw can follow, `tan(phi) <= 0.6 * bank_yaw_max_rate * v / turnGain`, never below 30 degrees (0.8 until the tuning round of 2026-10-17, see there).
- **Turn**: the yaw rate is fed forward from the bank, `omega = turnGain * tan(phi) / v` (`turnGain` learnt in steady turns from the ball integrator), and the ball loop (P + I) trims what is left. Bank is taken in the travel frame, before the head-check rotation.
- **Lift stays a force** (owner's decision, 2026-10-16): Lift is the vertical force and nothing else. Centre Lift is 1 g, zero vertical acceleration, so a climb or a sink carries on until the pilot stops it. No vertical speed estimate, no damping at the centre, no height hold. Considered and not taken: damping vertical speed at the centre (an accelerometer-integrated estimate, removed on 2026-10-15), Lift as a vertical speed command, and going back to the one-sided trim so the props' own damping returns. With the trim now matching the thrust to Lift, nothing else damps vertical speed either: the props make less thrust climbing and more sinking, and the trim takes that out too.
- **Speed: lower half a speed, upper half a forward force, brake in the bottom third** (2026-10-16): the owner found bank mode's top speed far below acro's (`webfpv-bank-20261016-165343.csv`: acro about 40 m/s at 50 to 65 degrees and full throttle; bank 19 to 22 m/s, the pitch target pinned at the 30 degree `bank_max_tilt_angle` with the motors near half). The forward tilt is now limited only by the thrust (with its 25 % reserve), and the old `bank_max_tilt_angle` became `bank_max_brake_angle`, which only limits tilting back to brake. The owner's design for the stick, from force over stick position:
  - lower half, a speed: 0 at the bottom to `bank_mid_speed` (new setting, default 0.50 g of drag) at the centre, feedforward + P. Only the bottom third (`BANK_BRAKE_STICK`) brakes: above it the P term only adds force, so a craft faster than the stick's speed gets the force that holds that speed and its own drag slows it, like coasting off the accelerator; a slower one is still driven up to it. Pulling the stick below a third when too fast is the brake.
  - upper half, a forward force: a straight line from the force the centre gives (the same P term, so the force over the stick is continuous at the centre whatever the speed) to the most forward force the trimmed thrust allows, lift * tan(tilt limit), at the top. No speed hold there.
  - How it got there: a first cut blended the top tenth of the stick to full force (the owner did not want the law to change near the top); then the speed was the stick times a `bank_max_speed` set above the top speed; then a mid speed with a computed top speed above it, where three quarters of the stick punched at near full power from the hover because the target sat above reach until the props lost thrust; the owner then chose a force for the upper half and braking only in the bottom third.
  - In the simulator, with Lift centred: full stick 40 m/s at 56 degrees, the same as a scripted acro pilot at full throttle (37 to 42 m/s); three quarters 35.5 m/s; half 19.6 m/s (0.43 g: the stick deadband and the P-only loop leave it short of 0.50); a quarter 6.8 m/s. From 40 m/s, the stick to 0.4 coasts to 20 m/s in 4 s and to its 14 m/s in about 10; to 0.3 brakes at 30 degrees to its 8.5 m/s in 2 s.
  - Still true at three quarters from the hover: the props have three times the forward force there that they have at top speed (4.4 g against 1.4 g), so a fraction of full force is a strong punch at first, as the same throttle is in acro. Raising the tilt cap alone flew the craft into the ground: at 40 m/s the props make a third of the modelled thrust, which needs the two-sided thrust trim below.
- **Collective from the measured tilt** (2026-10-16): Thrust = Lift / cos(measured tilt), never steeper than the thrust allows (so an attitude overshoot does not take the collective to full). It was set for the commanded tilt, and rolling into a bank at speed (the pitch target going from 50 to 0 degrees and the bank target from 0 to 80 at once) asked for the thrust of an 80 degree bank while the craft was still nearly level: 4 to 5 g up for a moment and 3 to 5 m/s more climb on every turn entry in the owner's flight `webfpv-bank-20261016-171738.csv`. Replayed through the new firmware, the turn entries add no climb. The thrust trim's model of the vertical force uses the measured tilt too.
- **Hard banks at speed** (2026-10-16, the owner's "janky between hard banks" flights `webfpv-bank-20261016-225549/230010/230106/230233.csv`): every flick of the Bank stick at speed nodded the pitch target 0 to 70 degrees and back, swung the nose 35 to 50 degrees off the track, kicked the climb up by 1.5 m/s and could drive the speed estimate negative (reversing). Fixed in this order, each measured with a scratch reversal sortie at 0.5 and 0.85 Speed:
  - the forward tilt is given what is left beside the steeper of the bank asked for and the bank the craft is still in (measured, travel frame), and it does not grow while the craft is rolling (the two banks more than 20 degrees apart), then at most 180 deg/s; it still falls at once (bank priority) and braking is not held. Pitch target spike through a reversal 70 degrees to 1.5, measured pitch excursion 38 to under 1.
  - the turn rate is fed forward from the bank the craft is in, not the one asked for: the track turns with the measured bank. The ball integrator and the turn-gain learner pause while rolling, as they do while the head check slews.

## Real hardware (2026-10-18)

The first test drone is the owner's BetaFPV Air65 R: board `BETAFPVG473_V2` (STM32G473, 512 KB flash), BMI270, a 1S ducted 65 mm whoop, with ELRS at 250 Hz. [air65-backup/reference.md](air65-backup/reference.md) has the drone's facts, the stock backup and how to restore it, the build and flash steps, the bench test and the log fields. Not yet flown in BANK.

Found while preparing it, open for the owner:
- **Flash gate**: `USE_BANK_MODE` needs `TARGET_FLASH_SIZE > 512` (`common_post.h`), which leaves the G473 out, so the drone is built with `OPTIONS="USE_BANK_MODE"`. With it the build uses 79 % of flash and 68 % of RAM. Whether the gate should take in 512 KB parts is a question for upstreaming.
- **Arming in BANK** (changed 2026-10-18, owner's call): the collective comes from Lift, so arming with BANK on and Lift centred spun straight up to hover power. Under BANK the arming check (`core.c`, `updateArmingStatus()`) now also wants the Lift (pitch) stick below `min_check`, the same test as the throttle stick, and sets `ARMING_DISABLED_THROTTLE` otherwise; unit test `ArmingPreventionTest.BankModeNeedsLiftStickLowToArm`. Armed that way the motors still run at the Lift floor's collective (about 11 %), not idle. Only the arming check changed: arming in ANGLE and switching to BANK in the air works as before, and disarm is unchanged. Not in the simulator (it does not compile `core.c`); not flown or bench tested yet.
- **Switching between BANK and ANGLE**: the throttle stick changes meaning, with nothing to bridge it. Leaving BANK with Speed at the bottom gives ANGLE zero throttle and the craft drops. Entering BANK at the hover throttle asks for a Speed near `bank_mid_speed`. For now the procedure is to switch only with the throttle stick near the hover position.
- **Runaway takeoff and the airmode latch read the Speed stick** (found 2026-10-18; fixed in tuning round 2, not flown yet: under BANK `processRx()` now reads the collective as the throttle percent, for the airmode latch, runaway takeoff's deactivation and launch control): both use `calculateThrottlePercentAbs()` in `core.c`, the throttle stick's percent, not the collective. After a launch in BANK, flying Speed in the lower half, runaway takeoff never switches itself off (it needs the stick above 20 % with a stick deflected, or above 40 %), so a 75 ms PID-sum spike while rotating disarms in mid-air. Airmode never latches, so Lift at the bottom resets the I-term. The fix is to use the collective as the throttle percent under BANK for both. Until then the hardware procedure sets `runaway_takeoff_prevention = OFF` (`air65-backup/restore-bank-firmware.txt`, pasted per `air65-backup/flashing.md`); whether to turn it back on with the fix is the owner's call.
- **Hooks never flown**: the throttle-low hooks in `core.c` and `failsafe.c` (I-term reset, PIDs at min throttle, auto-disarm, runaway takeoff, failsafe's landed timer) are not in the simulator, so the bench test and the first flights are their first run.
- **Plant differences**:
  - `bank_thrust_to_weight` is measured from a hover in ANGLE, as the hover throttle (reference.md has the method).
  - 1S sags much more than the simulator's pack (see "Battery sag" below).
  - The ducts change the rotor drag that the speed estimate and the ball are built on, including the side to front ratio behind the look-turn error. `bank_mid_speed` in g of drag will be a much lower real speed.
  - The accelerometer sees whoop vibration.
- **Logs**: `debug_mode = BANK` to blackbox on 2 MB of onboard flash. `bank-log.js` and its `--replay` read only the simulator's CSV, so hardware logs need a blackbox CSV converter (not written yet) before they can be read and replayed the same way.
